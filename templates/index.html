Kasama is a high school Filipino cultural club and nonprofit where students celebrate heritage, serve the neighborhood, and lead with bayanihan spirit. We create welcoming spaces through art, language, music, food, and mentorship.

Our Mission

We uplift Filipino identity with joy, empathy, and action by giving youth the tools to lead. Kasama centers culture, care, and community service so every student can feel seen and proud.

Culture

Workshops in folk dance, rhythms, and historic storytelling that keep traditions alive.

Leadership

Student-led committees build event planning, public speaking, and team leadership skills.

Service

Monthly outreach drives support families, elders, and newly arrived youth in our community.

Programs We Run

From after-school circles to citywide showcases, our programs are welcoming to all backgrounds.

Littles to Biggies

Older students mentor incoming members with guidance in academics, identity, and belonging.

Creative Studio

Visual art, spoken word, and dance labs inspired by Filipino stories and modern youth voices.

Dance Showcases

Volunteer-powered dance showcases performed at institutions and neighborhood groups.
