HACK-A-TON

Hack-A-Ton is a nonprofit that helps schools and communities start hackathons to cultivate the next generation of builders. We support STEM clubs, hands-on events, and mentorship so youth can build, connect, and innovate in person as well as online.

Our Mission

We foster innovators and everyday builders by bringing STEM education and hackathon experiences to youth. Hack-A-Ton centers community, mentorship, and hands-on events so students can solve real problems together.

STEM Workshops

Workshops in coding, prototyping, and hands-on projects that build practical skills.

Leadership

Student-led teams build event planning, project management, and leadership skills.

Community Impact

Community hackathons and outreach programs support local partners and student innovators.

Programs We Run

From school STEM clubs to community hackathons, our programs welcome students of all backgrounds.

STEM Clubs

Workshops in coding, prototyping, and design that help students build practical tech skills.

Mentorship

Student-led teams organize hackathons, develop leadership, and practice project management.

In-Person Events

Community hackathons and outreach programs connect youth with local partners to solve real challenges.
